\subsection{Purpose}
This document provides a comprehensive technical specification of the system previously defined in the Requirements Analysis and Specification Document (RASD). It evaluates the hardware and software architectures with particular emphasis on the modular interactions within the system. Additionally, the report details the methodologies for implementation, testing, and integration. Although the discourse is primarily technical and intended for developers, it serves as a valuable resource for stakeholders seeking insight into the system’s developmental lifecycle.
\subsection{Scope}
This design document specifies the system's functional behavior across both standard and critical operational scenarios. It further defines the architectural framework by detailing the logical allocation of components and their respective interdependencies.
